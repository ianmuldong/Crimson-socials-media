# Brand assets

- `logos/` – put the official Crimson VC logo here (transparent PNG or SVG preferred,
  e.g. `logos/crimson-logo.png`). Until it's added, templates use a drawn volleyball mark.
- `fonts/` – Anton (headlines) and Inter (body), bundled locally (SIL Open Font License).

Colours live in `src/brand/theme.ts`: crimson `#C8102E`, maroon `#5A0A16`, black `#070707`, white.
